Heritage & Monuments

Preserving the past

Andhra Pradesh has a rich cultural history, with archaeological sites, temples, historic structures and traditional art forms. Visitors should respect local customs and help protect heritage places.

LOCAL EXPERIENCES

Culture & Cuisine

  • Festivals: Sankranti, Ugadi and local celebrations.
  • Arts: Kalamkari, handloom traditions and classical dance.
  • Food: Regional rice dishes, spicy curries and traditional sweets.

TRAVEL SMART

Useful Travel Tips

  1. Check local weather before travelling.
  2. Respect local traditions and heritage rules.
  3. Keep public places clean and avoid littering.
  4. Confirm transport timings and entry details before visiting.
